My Career Choice: Financial Manager

I chose financial management because I like business, money, and leadership. I also think it would be cool helping companies make smart financial decisions and manage their money better. Since I live near Holly Springs, North Carolina, there are a lot of growing businesses and banks around the Raleigh area, so this career could be useful where I live too.

Learn more.

Average Salary

$156K

Financial managers usually make a lot of money because they help companies handle important financial decisions.


Job Outlook

This career is expected to grow over the next few years because businesses will always need help managing money.

Why I Am Interested In This Career

I think financial management sounds interesting because it mixes business, leadership, and problem solving together. I also like that you can work in many places like banks, companies, hospitals, or even sports organizations.

Job Responsibilities

  • Manage company budgets
  • Create financial reports
  • Help businesses save money
  • Plan investments
  • Make financial decisions

Education Needed

Most financial managers need a bachelor's degree in finance, accounting, or business. Some people also get an MBA later on.

Skills Needed

  • Leadership
  • Communication
  • Math skills
  • Problem solving
  • Organization

Knowledge Needed

  • Business and management
  • Customer service
  • Math like algebra, statistics, and geometry
  • English and communication skills
  • Basic understanding of laws and government rules

Important Abilities

  • Good speaking and listening skills
  • Problem solving and logical thinking
  • Using math to solve financial problems
  • Seeing patterns in numbers and data
  • Making smart decisions under pressure

Technology Used

  • Microsoft PowerPoint
  • Microsoft Excel
  • Oracle PeopleSoft
  • Workday software
  • Microsoft SQL Server

Ideal Personality Traits

  • Responsible
  • Confident
  • Focused
  • Good with pressure
  • Hardworking

Benefits

  • High salary
  • Good job opportunities
  • Can work in many industries
  • Leadership positions
  • Stable career

Challenges

  • Can be stressful sometimes
  • Important responsibilities
  • Long hours during deadlines
  • Need to stay updated on the economy

Best Locations For This Career

Financial managers are needed in places with a lot of businesses and banks.

Raleigh, NC

Raleigh is close to Holly Springs and has many growing businesses and financial companies.

Charlotte, NC

Charlotte is one of the biggest banking cities in the United States.

New York City

Many major banks and finance companies are located there.

Miami, FL

Miami has growing international businesses and financial companies.
